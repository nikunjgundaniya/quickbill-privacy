QuickBill POS – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

QuickBill POS ("QuickBill", "we", "the app") is a billing app for small food stalls, built by Nikunj Gundaniya (pbdev). This policy explains what data the app handles and what choices you have.

In short

  • Your menu, orders, bills and settings stay on your phone. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email or location.
  • After the free trial, free users see ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data for that. Pro users and trial users see no ads.

1. Data stored on your device

QuickBill works offline. The following is saved only in your phone's local storage:

  • Your menu, item prices, categories and table setup
  • Active orders and your history of paid bills
  • GST rate, table count and app settings
  • Trial start date and license key, activation date and duration

We cannot see this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted from your device. Back up regularly using Menu Editor → Data Backup.

2. Data you choose to share

When you share a bill or a backup, QuickBill opens your phone's share sheet so you can send it through WhatsApp, SMS, email or another app. What you send, and to whom, is your choice. That data is then handled by the app you share it with, under its own policy. Shared bills include a "Powered by QuickBill POS" footer.

3. License activation

License keys are verified entirely on your device. Activation does not contact our servers. If you buy a license by contacting us (for example on WhatsApp), we may see your phone number and message in that chat, and we use it only to issue your key and give support.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users (after the 30-day trial) see ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads on the Dashboard, History and Analytics screens, occasional interstitial ads after a completed bill, and optional rewarded ads. We never show ads on the Order or Bill screens, during onboarding, during the trial, or to Pro users.

To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process device and usage data, such as:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID, IP address, device and OS detailsServing and measuring ads, fraud prevention
Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)Ad performance and reporting
Approximate location derived from IPAd relevance

Ads may be personalized. You can turn off personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more in Google's advertising policy and Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

5. App usage analytics

We may record anonymous usage events (for example, app opened, onboarding completed, license screen viewed, ad shown) to understand how the app is used and to improve it. These events do not include your menu, bill contents, customer details or other business data.

6. App updates

QuickBill receives over-the-air updates through Expo (EAS Update). Checking for an update sends standard technical information, such as app version and device platform, to Expo's servers. See Expo's privacy policy.

7. Permissions

QuickBill needs internet access only for ads and app updates. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or storage.

8. Children

QuickBill is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Security and retention

Your business data lives on your device, so its security depends on your device's protection. The Menu Editor is PIN-protected, but we recommend also using a phone lock. We keep no copy of your business data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Data held by Google or Expo is kept under their policies.

10. Your choices

  • Delete your data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Limit ad personalization in your Android settings.
  • Remove ads by activating QuickBill Pro (₹299/year).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
